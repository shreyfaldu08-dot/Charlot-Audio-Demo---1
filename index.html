OUR PROJECTS

Common
Questions

Everything you need to know about our rentals, full-service production, and logistics.

Do you offer 'dry hire' rentals or just full production?

+

We offer both! For AV professionals and DJs, you can dry hire gear from our Charlotte warehouse. For corporate events, weddings, and concerts, we highly recommend our full-service production where our technicians handle delivery, setup, operation, and strike.

How far outside of Charlotte do you travel?

+

While our warehouse is based in Charlotte, NC, our production team regularly travels across the Southeast, including Raleigh, Asheville, Charleston, Atlanta, and beyond for large-scale events.

Is setup and strike included in the package price?

+

For our curated packages (Wedding, Corporate, Movie, DJ), standard delivery, setup, and strike within the Charlotte metro area are typically included. Complex rigs like flown line arrays or large LED walls will have itemized labor and logistics costs on your custom quote.

Do I need an on-site technician for my event?

+

For basic setups (like a microphone and a pair of speakers), we will show you how to operate it during setup. However, for any event involving multiple microphones, a band, complex lighting cues, or video switching, an on-site A1 (Audio Engineer) or V1 (Video Director) is required to guarantee a flawless show.
